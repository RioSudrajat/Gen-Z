## Guided Inquiry

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE realistic production-quality Gen-Z public Contact page design concept named Guided Inquiry. Target dimensions 1440 x 1100 px, desktop website viewport, no device or browser frame, no collage. Current date 20 October 2026; show no dates or invented metrics. Supplied current contact and homepage screenshots are visual references for existing logo, rounded geometric GenZ Display headings and GenZ UI body, brand and voice. Supplied existing operator photograph may be cropped into one supporting thumbnail. Do not copy existing form layout. Preserve exact Gen-Z identity; no new logo, invented people, serif typography, new identity, trust logos, team card, addresses or speed guarantees. White-only theme with white main canvas, dark navy-charcoal #111827 text, secondary #4B5563, deep teal #0F766E links, mint #2DD4BF button with dark text, #CCFBF1 restrained active surface. No dark page/panel, glow, glass, gradients, decorative dot grids, floating cards.
Design principle: guided application, a calm professionally crafted B2B intake journey. Show the asset operator path at the SECOND OF THREE steps, not multiple views in one picture. Single top header 72px: exact source Gen-Z logo left, Our purpose link and Launch app button right, normal current Contact Gen-Z text. At x100 y130 small Back to Gen-Z. Compact page heading 'Let’s discuss your asset.' 48px left-aligned and below 'A few details to help us prepare the right conversation.' Use 16px body text and persistent readable labels. Below heading, plain underlined topic choices 'Asset operator' selected, 'Investor', 'Partnership'.
Main area starts y320: narrow 280px navigation rail at left, vertical ordered stepper with thin connector and numbered step circles. First 'Your details' has checkmark and subtext 'Contact information', second 'Asset & financing' selected in deep teal bold, third 'Review inquiry' unselected. Active state uses number plus weight and teal so not color-only. Under steps a small 240x150 crop of supplied existing operator photograph with caption 'Illustrative infrastructure.' and plain helper 'We’ll review your inquiry before discussing next steps.' No outer card around this rail.
At right x480 a wide 760px white form stage not enclosed in a floating card. Above stage small 'Step 2 of 3' followed by 'About your asset' 34px heading. The form contains all of this step's fields: first row 'Asset category' native select placeholder 'Select category' (Energy / Infrastructure options hidden) and 'Operating status' native select placeholder 'Select status' (Operational, Maintenance, Planned, Not sure options hidden). Second row 'Country' shows fixed 'Indonesia' and 'Province / region' blank text input. Below full-width 'Asset description' visible label and 110px textarea placeholder 'Describe the asset, its operations and your role.' Below divider a heading 'Financing need (optional)' and amount input with IDR prefix, paired with unchecked 'Not sure yet' checkbox; no forced budget range. Under form bottom a subtle divider, a simple secondary 'Back' button at left and prominent mint 'Review inquiry' button at right. Both single-line text. No Submit button at this step and no fake success.
Clearly this is a three-step logical grouping: contact details exist on first step (Full name, Work email, Company optional, Phone optional); privacy acknowledgement occurs on final review step. Do not show those earlier/later fields on current view. Preserve Back ability and no timing/session pressure. Make page spacious but do not use giant dead space. Fields around 52px high, local radii 12px, clean slim borders with good contrast, regular type no uppercase tiny eyebrow. Bottom footer simple Gen-Z wordmark and existing tagline. Main content fits comfortably within canvas, full form and footer visible. Structure should feel more focused and polished than a conventional marketing contact form. Focus on hierarchy, spacing and brand fidelity, not ornament. Render crisp text without duplicated words or spelling errors.
```

## Role-led Workspace

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Generate ONE realistic, production-quality public Contact page design concept for Gen-Z, direction 'Role-led Workspace'. Target dimensions 1440 x 1220 px, naturally proportioned desktop webpage, no browser frame, no device, no collage or separate miniature screens. Current date 20 October 2026. Reference images supply existing Gen-Z logo, typography and asset-operator photo. Preserve this exact light-only brand: geometric rounded GenZ Display headings, GenZ UI sans body, white #FFFFFF canvas, #FAFAFA soft surfaces, dark #111827 primary and #4B5563 secondary text, #0F766E deep teal selection text, #2DD4BF mint with dark text for main action. No new brand, generic serif, Inter, gradient, glow, dark background, glass, purple, decorative dot grids, invented team/avatar/client logos/claims. Existing illustration photo may be reused once with honest caption. Carefully typeset readable 16px body and visible 15px labels, 52px controls, rounded 12px control corners, no outer app card.
This direction is DISTINCT from a scrolling form and a next/back wizard. It is an adaptive public contact workspace: visible audience selector, editable collapsible field groups, all within ONE page. No login requirement, no dashboard data, no issued inquiry or reference.
Top header 72px same contact identity: Gen-Z logo at x90, Our purpose and Launch app at right. Below y145 a left-aligned heading 'What would you like to discuss?' 54px with 'discuss?' deep teal. One short subtitle 'Choose a topic. Share the details that matter.' No hype.
Main content starts y290. At left a 280px wide role navigation on the open white canvas, title 'Your conversation'. Three VERTICAL selection buttons with clear radio markers and concise labels, separated by whitespace not three matching big cards. First selected 'Asset operator' dark teal and pale mint active background, supporting text 'Discuss an asset and its financing.' Second 'Investor' with subtext 'Discuss your investment mandate.' Third 'Partnership' with subtext 'Explore working with Gen-Z.' These are real audience choices not fake product feature cards. Under them one 250x180 crop of existing operator photograph and small caption 'Illustrative infrastructure.' Selection can change fields without destroying the other draft; do not show that explanation in UI.
Right x460 width870 is the form, header 'Asset inquiry' 32px. Below compact accordion sections with thin dividers and clear numbered labels indicating real form groups. First row CLOSED: '1  Your details', subtitle 'Name, work email, company and phone', right control 'Add details' and chevron; this section has NOT been completed, no checkmark or invented values. Second row OPEN: '2  Asset & financing', control 'Collapse'. In expanded section show: Asset category select 'Select category'; Operating status select 'Select status'; Country read-only text 'Indonesia' with NO dropdown chevron; Province / region blank input; Asset description textarea, placeholder 'Describe the asset, its operations and your role.'; Financing need (optional) with IDR amount field and unchecked Not sure yet checkbox. Use clean 2-column field rows but textarea full width. Third accordion row CLOSED '3  Review & privacy', subtitle 'Check your details before sending', right 'Review'. Beneath a 52px mint main button 'Review inquiry' aligned right or full width within form, no disabled fake success; it would validate and expand missing sections. A short helper beneath 'Please leave sensitive documents out of this form.'
The required privacy acknowledgement lives in Review & privacy before send. Contact group includes Full name and Work email, Company optional and Phone optional. These are not absent from the product, only collapsed in this static view. Make collapsed rows visually operable with text plus chevrons and keep semantic section numbers separate from decorative marketing labels. Inline form navigation can revisit any group; NO global next/back stepper, no progress bar, no wizard. Do not add saving capabilities, live support, booking calendar, guarantees or extra questions.
Bottom minimal footer matches Gen-Z with existing tagline and Back to Gen-Z. Frame must show all open fields, group rows and final action naturally with comfortable spacing and no crop. One clear focal point is the expanded asset form; selected role anchors meaning. Design with enough specificity and clarity to feel like an energy-infrastructure business tool rather than an agency contact template.
```
