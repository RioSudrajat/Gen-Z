## 2. Parallel Perspectives

Direction name: Parallel Perspectives. The STRUCTURE is two open equal editorial columns for the two audiences, both fully visible simultaneously. This must look substantially different from alternating rows: under the left-aligned section title and intro, start a two-column composition across the page, about 610px each separated by a 64px gap and optionally one subtle short vertical divider. No outer card backgrounds or card borders. Each column has small role label, then a large 16:9 editorial photo, then role headline, body and supporting material and its CTA. Left photo shows industrial rooftop solar and adjoining battery containers in a tropical Indonesian landscape, without people. Right photo shows a thoughtful analysis desk with contract papers and a closed laptop next to a large window overlooking the same type of solar infrastructure, no people, no fake dashboard. Treat both roles as equal, with same 8px-corner mint button style for "Discuss your asset" and "Discuss your mandate" and aligned button baseline. The role headlines should each fit 2-3 natural lines, not awkward word fragments. Categories are plain text, not chips. Entire two-column section fits naturally into 1440x1280 without clipping. It should feel like a precise premium energy-finance editorial, not two generic feature cards, not a pricing table. Plenty of white space and shared visual rhythm, teal role labels and existing Gen-Z typography. A small single caption "Illustrative infrastructure." below photographs is allowed.
