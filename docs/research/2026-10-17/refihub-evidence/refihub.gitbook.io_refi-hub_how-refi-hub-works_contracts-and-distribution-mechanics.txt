Every listing on ReFi Hub includes predefined contractual terms that govern how revenue is calculated, which costs if any are deducted, how cashflow is distributed, distribution frequency, and reporting cadence.
Distributions are based on actual asset performance. Forecasts may be provided for context, but only realised cashflow determines what Token Holders receive.
