ReFi Hub enables Token Holders to participate in solar infrastructure cashflow without sourcing, structuring, or managing assets themselves.
We do this by:
selecting and onboarding operating solar assets
providing the contractual and technical framework for funding rounds
defining clear distribution terms within each Token Sale & Investment Agreement
delivering transparent, on-chain reporting of production and Yield payments
ReFi Hub is solar infrastructure cashflow, accessed through modern on-chain rails.
