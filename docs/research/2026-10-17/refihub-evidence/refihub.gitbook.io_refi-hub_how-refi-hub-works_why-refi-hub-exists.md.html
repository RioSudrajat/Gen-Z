Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to page URLs; this page is available as [Markdown](https://refihub.gitbook.io/refi-hub/how-refi-hub-works/why-refi-hub-exists.md). # Why ReFi Hub Exists Global electricity demand is rising faster than energy systems and capital markets can adapt. Electrification, AI infrastructure, transport, and urban growth are placing sustained pressure on power grids worldwide. Much of the infrastructure needed to meet this demand sits outside traditional capital markets - in distributed projects, emerging regions, and operating assets that are too small or complex for institutional balance sheets. ReFi Hub enables capital to participate directly in operating solar infrastructure that produces measurable output, contractual revenue, and real-world cashflow - using on-chain systems to improve transparency and access, without changing how energy is generated, sold, or consumed.
