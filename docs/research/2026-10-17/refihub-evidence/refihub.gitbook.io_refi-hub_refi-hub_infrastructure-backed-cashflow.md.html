Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to page URLs; this page is available as [Markdown](https://refihub.gitbook.io/refi-hub/refi-hub/infrastructure-backed-cashflow.md). # Infrastructure-backed cashflow ReFi Hub connects capital with operating solar energy infrastructure and uses on-chain systems to distribute the cashflow those assets generate. We provide the infrastructure for projects to raise funding and manage Yield payments transparently, without changing how the underlying assets operate or what drives their economics. Yield on ReFi Hub comes from electricity being produced, customers paying for that energy, and contracts performing as agreed.
