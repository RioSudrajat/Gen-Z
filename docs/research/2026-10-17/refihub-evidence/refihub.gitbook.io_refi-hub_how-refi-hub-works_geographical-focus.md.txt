# Geographical Focus

ReFi Hub focuses on markets that offer favourable energy economics, regulatory clarity or established precedent, enforceable contracts, stable or manageable currency exposure, and the ability to build repeat operator relationships.

We currently focus on Brazil and Singapore solar, which offer distinct risk-return profiles within this framework.

Brazil offers high irradiance, strong energy economics, and established operator partnerships.

Singapore offers regulatory stability, premium REC pricing, and currency strength.
