Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to page URLs; this page is available as [Markdown](https://refihub.gitbook.io/refi-hub/how-refi-hub-works/transparency-and-reporting.md). # Transparency & Reporting ReFi Hub prioritises transparency over simplicity. Token Holders can expect regular reporting on asset performance, visibility into Yield distributions, historical cashflow data, and a clear distinction between forecasted and realised performance. On-chain systems are used to improve traceability, auditability, payout consistency, and reporting integrity.
