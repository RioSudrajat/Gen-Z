ReFi Hub curates assets against specific criteria through a three-stage review process:
Business fundamentals - team credibility, operational history, and jurisdiction
Project analysis - financial projections, equipment specifications, and market benchmarking
Risk assessment - collateral verification, legal enforceability, and ongoing monitoring access
Each project undergoes structured review, independent performance and contract verification, and financial analysis before approval for listing.
