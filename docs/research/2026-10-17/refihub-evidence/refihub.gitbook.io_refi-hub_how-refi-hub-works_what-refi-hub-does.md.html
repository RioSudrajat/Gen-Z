Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to page URLs; this page is available as [Markdown](https://refihub.gitbook.io/refi-hub/how-refi-hub-works/what-refi-hub-does.md). # What ReFi Hub does ReFi Hub enables Token Holders to participate in solar infrastructure cashflow without sourcing, structuring, or managing assets themselves. We do this by: * selecting and onboarding operating solar assets * providing the contractual and technical framework for funding rounds * defining clear distribution terms within each Token Sale & Investment Agreement * delivering transparent, on-chain reporting of production and Yield payments ReFi Hub is solar infrastructure cashflow, accessed through modern on-chain rails.
