ReFi Hub focuses on operating solar infrastructure that produces measurable environmental outcomes alongside cashflow. Impact is treated as a result of real-world activity, not as an abstract claim.
Measurable energy outcomes
All assets listed on ReFi Hub generate quantifiable outputs that can be measured and reported.
Core metrics typically include:
Electricity generated (kWh)
Asset uptime and utilisation
Displacement of higher-emissions grid power, where applicable
These same metrics underpin both operational reporting and impact assessment.
Solar infrastructure
Solar assets generate zero-emissions electricity through installed photovoltaic systems, displacing higher-carbon grid power over long operating lifetimes.
Impact is directly tied to energy delivered and real-world usage.
SDG alignment
ReFi Hub's asset focus aligns primarily with:
SDG 7 - Affordable and Clean Energy
SDG 9 - Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure
SDG 11 - Sustainable Cities and Communities
SDG 13 - Climate Action
Alignment is achieved through deployed infrastructure and delivered energy, not through offsets or unverified claims.
