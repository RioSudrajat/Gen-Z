ReFi Hub is designed for capital that prioritises cashflow quality over convenience, economic reality over abstraction, and durability over speed.
It suits investors who are comfortable with lower liquidity, longer time horizons, and returns that track real-world asset performance rather than market sentiment.
In exchange, investors gain direct exposure to cashflow that can be understood and traced back to operating infrastructure.
