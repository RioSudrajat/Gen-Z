FLP Solar Project 
Invoice & Revenue Summary
Project Identiﬁcation  
 
Project: FLP Solar Plant  
 
System Size: 267.85 kWp 
 
Location: Singapore 
 
Original Developer and PPA Counterparty: Renewables Capital Asia Pte. Ltd. (RCA) 
 
Designated Asset Holder: Pivot Green Asset Management Pte. Ltd. (PGAM) 
 
Economic Revenue Entitlement (from Revenue Commencement Date): PGAM
 
This document precedes the full invoice pack covering the operating period from September 2024 
to December 2025. 
It also includes the executed Term Sheet between RCA and PGAM, which sets out the agreed 
project transfer mechanics, revenue entitlement framework, and ownership structure applicable to 
this project within the Singapore solar portfolio.
 
Billing Structure
Each invoice reﬂects:
* Onsite solar electricity consumption billed at S$0.1250 per kWh
* Export of electricity to the grid at prevailing monthly export rates
* 9% Goods & Services Tax (GST)
* Itemised kWh consumption and cumulative metered generation
Monthly total invoice amounts (inclusive of GST) range approximately between S$2,000 and 
S$2,800.
Cumulative metered generation increases sequentially across the invoice history, evidencing 
continuous system operation.
 
Contracting Entity
All enclosed invoices are issued by:
Renewables Capital Asia Pte. Ltd. (RCA)
To
Freight Links Properties Pte. Ltd.
RCA is the original Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) counterparty and remains the invoicing 
entity until formal novation. 
Portfolio Transfer Framework 
The signed Term Sheet between RCA and Pivot Green Asset Management Pte. Ltd. (PGAM)  
provides that:
- The Freight Links project (267.85 kWp) forms part of the transferred portfolio (Clause 1.1).

ANNEX A -- REVENUE SHARING WATERFALL ALLOCATION STRUCTURE 
 
For the purposes of this Term Sheet, the “Revenue Sharing Waterfall” means the order of application of project 
revenues as set out in this ANNEX A. All project revenues of the RCA Projects shall be applied in accordance 
with the Revenue Sharing Waterfall, unless otherwise mutually agreed in writing by both PGAM and RCA. 
 
 
A. SCOPE OF APPLICATION:   
 
• This revenue sharing structure applies solely to the RCA Projects.  
 
B. REVENUE SOURCES  
• Feed-in tariffs, grid export payments, RECs’ sales, and all other project-related income from RCA Projects. 
 
C. REVENUE SHARING WATERFALL PRIORITY (FOR RCA PROJECTS)  
• All Project revenues shall be applied in the following order of priority, unless otherwise mutually agreed 
in writing by both PGAM and RCA: 
 
(1) Payment of the GAE Payables (as defined in Clause 1.2), until fully discharged. For avoidance of 
doubt, the GAE Payable is a one-time, disclosed obligation arising from works performed on the 
Transferred Projects, and is not treated as project financing debt. Its inclusion here ensures transparency 
and orderly settlement.  
 
(2) Statutory Charges and Regulatory Obligations  
• Payment of all statutory charges, licences, and regulatory obligations directly attributable to RCA 
Projects. 
• For avoidance of doubt, no PGAM corporate-level income tax or other group-level tax obligations 
shall be deducted at this stage, as such taxes are unrelated to the RCA’s Projects.  
 
(3) Agreed Debt Service Obligations (Senior Lenders) – if any  
• Repayment of principal and interest on all project financing from external banks or institutional 
lenders, duly approved in writing by both PGAM and RCA in respect of the RCA Projects. For clarity, 
this item covers only third-party external financing. It does not include amounts owed to GAE unless 
expressly approved in writing by both Parties in accordance with Clause 2.4. 
 
(4) Insurance Premiums (if applicable)  
• Annual insurance premiums required to maintain project compliance and risk coverage for RCA 
Projects. 
 
(5) Priority Return to Approved Investor (Preferred Equity) – if applicable  
• Any third-party investor (“Class A Investor”) may be granted a priority return to recover its invested 
capital and receive a mutually agreed benchmark return, solely from Net Distributable Revenues of 
the RCA Projects. 
 
• All such distributions shall be made only after sa tisfaction of higher-ranking Revenue Sharing 
Waterfall items. 
 
• Optional Capital Recovery Structure (if mutually agreed): Alternatively, Class A Investor may 
receive 100% of the distributable cashflows of RCA Projects until it has recovered its invested capital 
plus the mutually agreed benchmark return.   
• Thereafter, distributions shall commence. 
 
(5) O&M and Compliance Costs   
• Payment of O&M and compliance costs, including equipment servicing, site works, performance 
monitoring, regulatory compliance for RCA Projects.   
• General administrative expenses and corporate overhead shall not be included in this category, except:  
i. where such costs are solely and directly attributable to the RCA Projects, are incurred at market-
standard rates, and expressly approved in writing by PGAM and RCA; or
