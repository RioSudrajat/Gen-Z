1.3 REVENUE RECOGNITION  
• Revenue accrues to PGAM: only from [1 September 2025] (the “Revenue Commencement Date”).  
• RCA shall retain all revenues relating to the period up to 31 August 2025, including August invoices 
issued in September and payments received thereafter.  
• Any RECs attributable to generation prior to 1 September 2025 shall remain RCA ’s entitlement, with 
RECs thereafter accruing to PGAM on a pro-rata basis.  
• For avoidance of doubt, all revenues attributable to the solar-generated electricity supplied to the clients 
under the Transferred Projects on or after the Revenue Commencement Date, shall belong to PGAM, 
regardless of the date of invoicing, receipt of payment, or execution of the relevant Novation Deed (each 
a “Novation Deed”, and together the “Novation Deeds”) to be entered into between RCA, PGAM, and 
the relevant counterparty.  
• Until the relevant Novation Deed for a Transferred Project is fully executed, RCA shall remain the legal 
counterparty under the PPA for that Transferred Project, with all rights and obligations vis-à-vis the 
client, and in that capacity, shall continue to issue invoices and receive payments in its own name, but 
shall hold such revenues on bare trust for PGAM and promptly remit them to PGAM (or its nominated 
account) within three (3) business days of receipt, failing which interest shall accrue at one percent (1%) 
per month on overdue sums until payment.  
• If the relevant Novation Deed of any Project is not completed by 31 October 2025 (the “Longstop Date”), 
the Parties shall execute an Assignment of Receivables Agreement between RCA (as assignor) and 
PGAM (as assignee) to give legal effect to the interim revenue pass-through arrangement described 
above, thereby ensuring PGAM’s entitlement to all revenues from such Project is legally enforceable 
until novation is completed. RCA will bear the costs of completing each Novation Deed. 
 
1.4 ASSET MANAGEMENT COOPERATION  
• If mutually agreed, RCA may provide ongoing operational & technical support (including, if required, 
invoicing, revenue collection and related administration) under a separate Service Agreement, if and 
when executed by the Parties. Such agreement may may specify:  
i. A mutually agreed management or service fee (fixed, cost-plus, or otherwise structured); 
ii. Treatment of pass-through O&M and compliance costs (with prior mutual approval required for 
extraordinary expenses);  
iii. Mutually agreed reporting obligations relevant to the Transferred Projects; and 
iv. Additional consideration beyond RCA’s thirty percent (30%) revenue entitlement, where RCA 
personnel are materially and directly responsible for originating an opportunity, securing land or 
building rights, obtaining material permits, or introducing/negotiating with any offtakers or 
financiers. Such consideration may take the form of (i) reimbursement of secondment costs, and/or 
(ii) a mutually agreed success or development fee. 
 
 
2. REVENUE ENTITLEMENT   
 
2.1 OWNERSHIP OF PROJECTS 
 
• PGAM shall hold legal ownership of the Transferred Projects and any Future Projects novated by RCA 
(together, the “RCA Projects”), with effect from the Effective Date of the relevant Novation Deed duly 
executed by RCA, PGAM and acknowledge by the relevant PPA counterparty (client). Only upon such 
execution shall PGAM assume all rights and obligations under the relevant Project. The Parties shall 
use best efforts to complete execution of each Novation Deed no later than 31 October 2025. Until such 
execution, RCA shall maintain in force the Assignment of Receivables in favour of PGAM to secure all 
revenues and shall continue to perform all acts necessary to give effect to PGAM’s beneficial ownership 
of the relevant Project.
