3.2 CONFIDENTIALITY

3.3 TERM & SURVIV AL  
• This Term Sheet shall remain in effect from the Effective Date until the expiry or termination of the last 
of the RCA Projects under its respective PPA.  
• For avoidance of doubt, Clause 3.2 (Confidentiality), and Clause 3.5 (Governing law and dispute 
resolution) shall remain binding on the Parties even after such expiry or termination, to the extend 
necessary to give effect to this Term Sheet. 
 
3.4 PORTFOLIO RECOGNITION  
• The Parties acknowledge that RCA may, for marketing, fundraising, and corporate track record purposes, 
reference the RCA Projects as part of its portfolio to reflect its originating role, and such reference do 
not expressly suggest ongoing ownership or involvement after the transfer, unless otherwise agreed in 
writing. 
 
3.5 GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION  
• This Term Sheet shall be governed by the laws of Singapore.  
• Any dispute shall first be referred to Singapore Mediation Centre (SMC) for resolution in good faith.  
• If not resolved within thirty (30) days, either Party may refer the dispute to the courts of Singapore, 
which shall have exclusive jurisdiction for enforcement purposes.
