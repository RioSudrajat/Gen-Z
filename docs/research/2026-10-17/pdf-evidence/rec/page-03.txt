1.2 DEBT & LIABILITY  
 
• The Transferred Projects shall be made available free from any  existing financial indebtedness or  
obligations of debt -like nature,  including but not limited to any bank loans, shareholder loans, 
guarantees, bonds, or other borrowings, tax arrears, fines, penalties, litigation claims, legacy obligations 
and similar liabilities (collectively, “Excluded Liabilities”).  
 
• The only exception to this is the outstanding amount payable to GAE for works performed on the 
Transferred Projects, together with any contractual interest accruing thereon (the “GAE Payable”). For 
clarity, the GAE Payable constitutes a bona fide trade payables incurred in the ordinary course of project 
construction, and does not represent a loan, borrowing, or financial indebtedness. 
 
• RCA warrants that , apart from the GAE Payable, there are no other debts , liabilities, or obligations 
relating to the Transferred Projects that could transfer to PGAM. All other historical obligations remain 
with RCA.
