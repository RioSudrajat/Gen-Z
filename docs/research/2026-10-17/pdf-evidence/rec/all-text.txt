Introductory Note: 
This document covers the Renewable Energy Certiﬁcates (RECs) issued for the Freight Links 
Properties (FLP) solar project and the executed Term Sheet governing the project’s transfer and 
revenue structure. 
RECs are issued in the name of Renewables Capital Asia Pte. Ltd. (RCA), as the original developer 
and legal counterparty under the Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) at the time of issuance.
 
The attached Term Sheet between RCA and Pivot Green Asset Management Pte. Ltd. (PGAM) 
sets out the agreed transfer structure, including the allocation of revenue and environmental 
attributes from the Revenue Commencement Date. 
A total of 433 RECs were issued in 2024 at an agreed price of SGD 65 per REC, representing SGD 
28,145 (ex GST) in aggregate value.
.
This package documents:
- Historical REC issuance under RCA
- The transfer framework to PGAM
- The agreed allocation of economic rights from the Revenue Commencement Date

provided as a high-level overview and should be read together with the full underlying
documentation, including the complete invoice set and executed Term Sheet. In the event of any
inconsistency, the original source documents shall prevail.

TERM SHEET FOR PROJECT TRANSFER & REVENUE SHARIING 
 
 
This term sheet (“ Term Sheet ”) outlines the principal terms and conditions in connection with the proposed 
transfer of the Singapore solar project (the “Transferred Projects”), and any future novated project (s) (each a 
“Project” and collectively the “Projects”), 
From 
 
RENEWABLES CAPITAL ASIA PTE. LTD. (Company Registration No.202144830E), a company registered 
in Singapore with its registered office address at 182 Cecil Street, #17, Frasers Tower, Singapore 069547  
(“RCA”),  
To 
 
PIVOT GREEN ASSET MANAGEMENT PTE. LTD. (Company Registration No.202516002E), a company 
registered in Singapore with its registered office address at 23 new industrial road, #05 -04, Solstice Business 
Center, Singapore 536209, being designated to hold the RCA Projects under this Term Sheet (“PGAM”),  
 
Together, the “Parties” and each a “Party”.  
 
Background: 
RCA has historically undertaken the development, financing, and/or construction of the Transferred Projects with 
certain works performed by its appointed contractor, Global Energy Alliance Pte. Ltd. (“GAE”). For the avoidance 
of doubt, GAE is a contractor to RCA in connection with the Projects and in not a party to this Term Sheet. 
 
This Term Sheet records the principles agreed between the Parties in connection with the novation of the 
Transferred Projects and related arrangements.

1.2 DEBT & LIABILITY  
 
• The Transferred Projects shall be made available free from any  existing financial indebtedness or  
obligations of debt -like nature,  including but not limited to any bank loans, shareholder loans, 
guarantees, bonds, or other borrowings, tax arrears, fines, penalties, litigation claims, legacy obligations 
and similar liabilities (collectively, “Excluded Liabilities”).  
 
• The only exception to this is the outstanding amount payable to GAE for works performed on the 
Transferred Projects, together with any contractual interest accruing thereon (the “GAE Payable”). For 
clarity, the GAE Payable constitutes a bona fide trade payables incurred in the ordinary course of project 
construction, and does not represent a loan, borrowing, or financial indebtedness. 
 
• RCA warrants that , apart from the GAE Payable, there are no other debts , liabilities, or obligations 
relating to the Transferred Projects that could transfer to PGAM. All other historical obligations remain 
with RCA.

1.3 REVENUE RECOGNITION 
 
• Revenue accrues to PGAM: only from [1 September 2025] (the “Revenue Commencement Date”). 
 
• RCA shall retain all revenues relating to the period up to 31  August 2025, including August invoices 
issued in September and payments received thereafter. 
 
• Any RECs attributable to generation prior to 1  September 2025 shall remain RCA ’s entitlement, with

• For avoidance of doubt, all revenues attributable to the solar-generated electricity supplied to the clients 
under the Transferred Projects on or after the Revenue Commencement Date, shall belong to PGAM, 
regardless of the date of invoicing, receipt of payment, or execution of the relevant Novation Deed (each 
a “Novation Deed”, and together the “Novation Deeds”) to be entered into between RCA, PGAM, and 
the relevant counterparty. 
 
• Until the relevant Novation Deed for a Transferred Project is fully executed, RCA shall remain the legal 
counterparty under the PPA  for that Transferred Project, with all rights and obligations vis-à-vis the 
client, and in that capacity, shall continue to issue invoices and receive payments in its own name, but 
shall hold such revenues on bare trust for PGAM and promptly remit them to PGAM (or its nominated 
account) within three (3) business days of receipt, failing which interest shall accrue at one percent (1%) 
per month on overdue sums until payment. 
 
• If the relevant Novation Deed of any Project is not completed by 31 October 2025 (the “Longstop Date”), 
the Parties shall execute an Assignment of Receivables Agreement between RCA (as assignor) and 
PGAM (as assignee) to give legal effect to the interim revenue pass -through arrangement described 
above, thereby ensuring PGAM’s entitlement to all revenues from such Project is legally enforceable 
until novation is completed. RCA will bear the costs of completing each Novation Deed. 
 
1.4 ASSET MANAGEMENT COOPERATION 
 
• If mutually agreed, RCA may provide ongoing operational & technical support (including, if required, 
invoicing, revenue collection and related administration)  under a separate Service Agreement , if and 
when executed by the Parties. Such agreement may may specify: 
 
i. A mutually agreed management or service fee (fixed, cost-plus, or otherwise structured); 
ii. Treatment of pass-through O&M and compliance costs  (with prior mutual approval required for 
extraordinary expenses);  
iii. Mutually agreed reporting obligations relevant to the Transferred Projects; and 
iv. Additional consideration beyond RCA’s thirty percent (30%) revenue entitlement, where RCA 
personnel are materially and directly responsible for originating an opportunity, securing land or 
building rights, obtaining material permits, or introducing/negotiating with  any offtakers or 
financiers. Such consideration may take the form of (i) reimbursement of secondment costs, and/or 
(ii) a mutually agreed success or development fee. 
 
 
2. REVENUE ENTITLEMENT   
 
2.1 OWNERSHIP OF PROJECTS 
 
• PGAM shall hold legal ownership of the Transferred Projects and any Future Projects novated by RCA 
(together, the “RCA Projects”), with effect from the Effective Date of the relevant Novation Deed duly 
executed by RCA, PGAM and acknowledge by the relevant PPA counterparty (client). Only upon such 
execution shall PGAM assume all rights and obligations under the relevant Project.  The Parties shall 
use best efforts to complete execution of each Novation Deed no later than 31 October 2025. Until such 
execution, RCA shall maintain in force the Assignment of Receivables in favour of PGAM to secure all 
revenues and shall continue to perform all acts necessary to give effect to PGAM’s beneficial ownership 
of the relevant Project.

2.2 REVENUE SHARE & DISTRIBUTION 
 
• RCA shall be entitled to thirty percent (30%) of the Net Distributable Revenues of RCA Projects, with 
all calculations and distributions to be made in accordance with the Revenue Sharing Waterfall set out 
in ANNEX A. 
 
• PGAM shall apply such revenues in accordance with the agreed  Revenue Sharing Waterfall and shall 
remit RCA ’s thirty percent (30%) revenue share promptly following each  distribution declared by 
PGAM, and in any event no later than thirty (30) calendar days, or such other period as may be mutually 
agreed by the Parties in writing.  
 
• Distributions shall be made only out of positive Net Distributable Revenues and shall not be guaranteed 
or scheduled on fixed dates. For administrative purposes, distribution may, at PGAM’s discretion, be 
declared and made on a quarterly or semi-annual basis, after reconciling project revenues and Revenue 
Sharing Waterfall items.  Distributions shall be declared whenever sufficient cash is available  for 
distribution under ANNEX A.  
 
• Whenever PGAM declares a distribution, it shall at the same time provide RCA with a  distribution 
statement evidencing (i) revenues received, (ii) payments made under Revenue Sharing Waterfall items 
(1) to (5), and (iii) any resulting Net Distributable Revenues. For avoidance of doubt, RCA ’s rights to 
access revenue and Revenue Sharing Waterfall records are set out in Clause 2.5. 
 
• Any dispute over any calculation of the Net Distributable Revenues shall be resolved in good faith and, 
if not resolved, referred  to an independent accountant mutually appointed by the Parties, whose 
determination shall be used as the basis for settlement, without prejudice to either Party’s rights under 
Clause 3.4. 
 
• For avoidance of doubt: 
i. no PGAM corporate-level overhead or general administrative expenses, or unrelated general costs 
not directly attributable to RCA Projects  shall be deducted prior to the calculation of Net 
Distributable Revenues; and 
ii. only the agreed Revenue Sharing Waterfall items set out in ANNEX A shall apply.  
 
2.3 FUTURE PROJECT PIPELINE & NOVATION 
 
• RCA may from time to time novate to PGAM additional solar projects in Singapore (the “Future 
Projects”), which PGAM shall accept subject to its customary internal approvals.  
 
• Thes same revenue entitlement (70% PGAM : 30% RCA), together with the Revenue Sharing Waterfall 
and calculation methodology set out in ANNEX A , shall apply mutatis mutandis , unless otherwise 
amended by mutual agreement of the Parties.  
 
• The novation mechanism, timing, and documentation of novation (for both the Transferred Projects and 
any Future Projects) shall be effected by the execution of a Novation Deed (or such other formal 
documentation as required the law), following written notice by RCA to PGAM and the relevant client.  
 
• For the five (5) Transferred Projects set out in Clause 1.1, RCA shall bear all  third-party legal and 
administrative costs at market-standard rates directly attributable to the novation or transfer.  
 
• For any Future Projects novated to PGAM, such third -party legal and administrative costs  shall be 
shared pro rata between PGAM and RCA, or as otherwise mutually agreed.  
 
• Each Party shall bear its own internal costs relating to the novation process. 
 
2.4 PROJECT FINANCING 
 
• PGAM shall be solely responsible for arranging and securing all project financing and refinancing for 
RCA Projects.  
 
• RCA shall not be required to  provide any guarantees or security in connection with such financing or 
refinancing, unless it voluntarily elects to do so in its sole discretion. 
 
• PGAM shall not enter into any financing arrangements that would subordinate or dilute or otherwise  
materially affect RCA’s revenue entitlement or risk exposure without RCA’s prior written consent. RCA 
shall respond to any such request within ten (10) business days of receipt of full details of the proposed 
financing. Such consent shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed, and PGAM may proceed if 
RCA ’s consent is unreasonably withheld.

2.5. TRANSPARENCY & REPORTING 
 
• For administrative purposes and a t PGAM’s discretion, the mechanism for revenue recording may be 
(i) a separate project bank account for the RCA Projects; or (ii) on-book segregation of all revenues and 
expenses relating to the RCA  Projects, or (iii) any other recording approach that is transparent and 
auditable. The chosen approach shall be applied from the Revenue Commencement Date so that all 
revenues and expenses are easily identifiable, and if required, capable of verification for the purposes 
of applying the Revenue Sharing Waterfall. For clarity, this provision relates only to transparency of 
revenues and expenses for application of Revenue Sharing Waterfall, and is independent of any legal 
form or ownership structure through which the Transferred Projects are held. 
 
• RCA will have  access, as reasonably requested , to any periodic  statements or equivalent records , 
consistent with PGAM’s internal reporting practices, showing project revenues received and their 
applications under the Revenue Sharing Waterfall. 
 
• PGAM will share with RCA  periodic financial information, as and when available under PGAM’s 
internal reporting cycle,  and in any case no less than once annually, evidencing project revenues, 
deductions under the Revenue Sharing Waterfall , and cash available for distribution, whether or not a 
distribution is declared.  
 
• RCA may, in good faith consultation with PGAM, request an independent review (or, if mutually agreed, 
an audit) of the revenues and applications under the Revenue Sharing Waterfall for all RCA Projects, at 
RCA’s cost.  
 
2.6. REPURCHASE OPTION 
 
• RCA may, but shall not be obliged to, submit a written request to repurchase the RCA Projects (whether 
consisting of one or more individual projects, or the entire portfolio) from PGAM, upon giving not less 
than sixty (60) days’ written notice, or any such longer period as may be required to obtain any lender 
consents required by PGAM’s financing arrangement, if applicable.  
 
• PGAM shall consider such request in good faith and shall have discretion to determine whether to accept 
it, while also discussing the repurchase request with RCA before making its decision, and  shall use 
reasonable endeavours to procure any required lender consents without delay and promptly inform RCA 
of any such requirements and its decision.  
 
• If a repurchase is agreed, the repurchase price shall equal: 
i. the outstanding principal and interest of any project financing amounts, and 
ii. any documented, third -party project costs directly incurred by PGAM for the RCA Projects that 
remain unrecovered, excluding PGAM corporate-level overhead or general administrative  
 
• In addition, the Parties may mutually agree on an appropriate additional premium on the repurchase price, 
not to exceed five percent (5%) of the repurchase price unless otherwise expressly agreed by the Parties, 
having regard to prevailing market practice for comparable rooftop solar project transactions.

ANNEX A -- REVENUE SHARING WATERFALL ALLOCATION STRUCTURE 
 
For the purposes of this Term Sheet, the “Revenue Sharing Waterfall” means the order of application of project 
revenues as set out in this ANNEX A. All project revenues of the RCA Projects shall be applied in accordance 
with the Revenue Sharing Waterfall, unless otherwise mutually agreed in writing by both PGAM and RCA. 
 
 
A. SCOPE OF APPLICATION:  
 
 
• This revenue sharing structure applies solely to the RCA Projects.  
 
B. REVENUE SOURCES 
 
• Feed-in tariffs, grid export payments, RECs’ sales, and all other project-related income from RCA Projects. 
 
C. REVENUE SHARING WATERFALL PRIORITY (FOR RCA PROJECTS) 
 
• All Project revenues shall be applied in the following order of priority, unless otherwise mutually agreed 
in writing by both PGAM and RCA: 
 
(1) Payment of the GAE Payables (as defined in Clause 1.2), until fully discharged. For avoidance of 
doubt, the GAE Payable is a one -time, disclosed obligation arising from works performed on the 
Transferred Projects, and is not treated as project financing debt. Its inclusion here ensures transparency 
and orderly settlement.  
 
(2) Statutory Charges and Regulatory Obligations 
 
• Payment of all statutory charges, licences, and regulatory obligations  directly attributable to RCA 
Projects. 
• For avoidance of doubt, no PGAM corporate-level income tax or other group-level tax obligations 
shall be deducted at this stage, as such taxes are unrelated to the RCA’s Projects.  
 
(3) Agreed Debt Service Obligations (Senior Lenders) – if any 
 
• Repayment of principal and interest on all project financing from external banks or institutional 
lenders, duly approved in writing by both PGAM and RCA in respect of the RCA Projects. For clarity, 
this item covers only third-party external financing. It does not include amounts owed to GAE unless 
expressly approved in writing by both Parties in accordance with Clause 2.4. 
 
(4) Insurance Premiums (if applicable) 
 
• Annual insurance premiums required to maintain project compliance and risk coverage  for RCA 
Projects. 
 
(5) Priority Return to Approved Investor (Preferred Equity) – if applicable 
 
• Any third-party investor (“Class A Investor”) may be granted a priority return to recover its invested 
capital and receive a mutually agreed benchmark return, solely from Net Distributable Revenues of 
the RCA Projects. 
 
• All such distributions shall be made only after sa tisfaction of higher -ranking Revenue Sharing 
Waterfall items. 
 
• Optional Capital Recovery Structure (if mutually agreed): Alternatively, Class A Investor may 
receive 100% of the distributable cashflows of RCA Projects until it has recovered its invested capital 
plus the mutually agreed benchmark return.  
 
• Thereafter, distributions shall commence. 
 
(5) O&M and Compliance Costs  
 
• Payment of O&M and compliance costs, including equipment servicing, site works, performance 
monitoring, regulatory compliance for RCA Projects.  
 
• General administrative expenses and corporate overhead shall not be included in this category, except: 
 
i. where such costs are solely and directly attributable to the RCA Projects, are incurred at market-
standard rates, and expressly approved in writing by PGAM and RCA; or

ii. any management fees, administrative support fees, or seconded personnel costs payable to RCA 
(or its affiliates) under a separate service or secondment agreement mutually agreed by the 
Parties. 
 
• Essential O&M Protection: Essential safety-critical, compliance -related O&M related to RCA 
Projects shall not be deferred and must be funded as a priority before investor distributions.

(6) Net Distributable Revenues:

D. DEFINITION OF NET DISTRIBUTABLE REVENUES 
 
• “Net Distributable Revenues ” means all project -related revenues  of RCA Projects  remaining after 
satisfaction of items (1) to (5) above, expressly excluding: 
 
i. any PGAM corporate overhead, administrative charges, or general costs not directly attributable to 
the RCA Projects, except expressly approved under item (5); and 
ii. any non-cash accounting charges (including depreciation, provisions, or reserve).
