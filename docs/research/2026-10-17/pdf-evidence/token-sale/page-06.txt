TERM DEFINITION Fixed Payment Component means the portion of the Yield payment which is the fixed monthly amount payable by the Recipient to Token Holders on each Payment Date, as specified in Schedule 1, which constitutes an unconditional contractual payment obligation of the Recipient, independent of Project performance or Gross Revenue. Forks is defined in section 3.8. of this Agreement. Funding Round is the tokenized Funding Round that the Recipient raises for, pursuant to the terms of this Agreement.  Grace Period the period of five (5) business days following the due date of any portion of any Yield Payment during which non-payment shall not result in penalties as further Schedule 1. Indemnified Party is defined in section 18 of this Agreement. Indemnifying Party is defined in section 18 of this Agreement. Investment shall mean the Investment represented by Tokens and provided by the Token Holder as further described in Section 3 and Schedule 1 of this Agreement, which shall be valid and enforceable only until the End of Term.  Investment Amount is defined in Schedule 1. KYC Process is defined in section 9 of this Agreement. KYC/AML & CTF Policy shall mean the policies and procedures implemented by the Platform to detect and prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and corruption. Materials shall mean any whitepaper, content or documents issued or produced by the Recipient related to Tokens or Investments, including the Recipient’s website, and the information and documents about the Recipient, including any materials made available via the Platform. Money means the money or currency of any country or jurisdiction that is: a) designated as legal tender; and, b) circulated, customarily used, and accepted as a medium of exchange in the country or jurisdiction of issuance. Gross Revenue means, for any relevant period, all amounts of cash actually received by the Recipient from: (a) the sale of electricity generated by the Project; and (b) any production-based environmental attributes, renewable energy certificates, carbon credits, or other performance-linked incentives directly attributable to the generation and sale of electricity by the Project, where applicable, calculated in accordance with Schedule 1.
