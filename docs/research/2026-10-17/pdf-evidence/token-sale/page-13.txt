3.14)  The Tokens represent a fractional right to Yield payment arising from the Project, as described in Schedule 1. No legal assignment of receivables is created except as expressly perfected under applicable local law.  Such payments shall be derived from the sale of the Project’s energy generation, as well as any associated environmental attributes, including Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs), carbon credits, or subsidies where applicable, as described in Section 1, and distributed proportionally to each Token Holder’s total Token Holdings. To the extent Environmental Attributes (including Renewable Energy Certificates) are included in Gross Revenue, their recognition, deemed pricing (if any), reconciliation, and distribution mechanics shall be governed exclusively by Schedule 1. 3.15)  On each Payment Date, from the First Repayment Date until the End of Term (inclusive), the Recipient shall pay to the Smart Contract a single Aggregate Payment in Stablecoin (USDC) comprising both: (i) the Yield payable to Token Holders; and (ii) the Platform Servicing Fee. The value of this Aggregate Payment is laid out in Schedule 1. The Platform Servicing Fee is mechanically deducted via Smart Contract and does not constitute brokerage, advisory, or discretionary compensation. 3.16)  Where Schedule 1 provides for a Fixed Payment Component, the Recipient’s obligation to pay such Fixed Payment Component shall be absolute and unconditional, and shall not be contingent upon Gross Revenue, Project performance, or operational status of the Project. 3.17)  In the event that the Project, or any component thereof, becomes non-functional, damaged, or is otherwise unable to operate for any reason (“Breakdown Event”), the Recipient shall be solely responsible for the diagnosis, repair, and full cost of restoring the Project to operational status, including any required replacement parts, technician labour, hardware, or software. For the avoidance of doubt, any maintenance, repair, or restoration costs arising from a Breakdown Event shall not be deducted from Gross Revenue and shall not reduce the Yield owed to Token Holders. Such costs shall be borne entirely by the Recipient. In no case shall the Token Holders be liable, directly or indirectly, for any cost, expense, or obligation arising from a Breakdown Event. 3.18)  The Recipient shall ensure that the payment of Yield is made to the Smart Contract with the requisite amounts on or before each Payment Date. The Recipient shall also publish summaries of Project Gross Revenue via the Platform dashboard or an email notice to Token Holders, on request, up to a maximum of on a quarterly basis.  3.19)  Reflections of Prior Project performance, and/or case studies of yield from similar projects may be laid out in the Recipient’s Materials, however these estimates in no way reflect any guarantee from the Recipient, are not legally binding and could be affected by a number of reasons including usership of the Project, unforeseen circumstances or any other reason such as those mentioned within the Disclaimers.  3.20)  Yield shall be paid on the Payment Date, monthly, from the First Repayment Date until the end of Term. The Recipient’s obligation to pay Yield shall automatically terminate upon the End of Term, and Token Holders expressly waive any claim to Yield after such date. For the avoidance of doubt, nothing in this Section limits the survival of accrued but unpaid Obligations, including penalties, costs or indemnities, or claims for prior breach, as expressly preserved under the definition of End of Term. 3.21)  If the Recipient fails to make any Yield payments on the relevant due date, a “Penalty” shall accrue at the rate laid out in Schedule 1, provided that the Recipient shall have five (5) Business Days to rectify any unpaid Obligations from the Payment Date before such Penalty is applicable.  3.22)  In addition, if such failure to pay as described in 3.20 continues for more than 60 Business Days, or if an Event of Default is declared for any other reason, the Recipient shall owe the Token Holder a “Default Compensation Amount”, as laid out in Schedule 1.  3.23)  The Recipient shall supply the Platform (and, upon request, Token Holders) with an API endpoint or verifiable CSV report delivering meter-verified kWh data, purchase invoices, revenue statements, or other similar proofs for the preceding month, quarterly, or on demand by the Platform. The Recipient shall supply these data, no later than thirty (30) Business Days after a request is made. Failure to comply within sixty (60) Business Days after written notice shall constitute an Event of Default.
