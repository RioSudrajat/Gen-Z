TERM DEFINITION Project shall mean the entire solar photovoltaic generation facility that is being constructed or financed, in whole or in part, through the Funding Round. For clarity, “Project” includes the entire facility, regardless of whether any portion is not funded through the Funding Round. Purchase Price shall mean the price per Token purchased by a Token Holder, as provided in Schedule 1. Recipient shall, where the context so permits or requires, mean PIVOT GREEN ASSET MANAGEMENT PTE LTD or its subsidiaries and Affiliated Entities and their successors and assigns. Relevant Date is defined in section 14.ee) of this Agreement. Revenue Support Payment  means any payment made by the Recipient to the Smart Contract to supplement Project revenues where the Aggregate Payment derived from Gross Revenue would otherwise be insufficient to meet the Target Aggregate Payment described in Schedule 1. Revenue Support Payments shall constitute an absolute and unconditional payment obligation of the Recipient when triggered in accordance with Schedule 1 and shall form part of the Aggregate Payment for the relevant Payment Date. Smart Contract means the self-executing code deployed by the Recipient using the Platform’s software infrastructure, that handles the entire flow of funds during the raise, and the payments of any Obligations. Soft Cap means the minimum amount of Stablecoins that must be raised using the Platform’s software infrastructure, before the raise is considered successful, and the Recipient is entitled to receive the Investment Amount. Stablecoins Refers to USD-denominated digital settlement tokens (such as USDC), which are used purely as a payment and accounting mechanism for Yield participations, and not offered or intermediated by the Platform. Target Aggregate Payment means the minimum Aggregate Payment required to be delivered by the Recipient to the Smart Contract on each Payment Date, as specified in Schedule 1. Token Holder Wallet Access Credentials is defined in section 5.5 of this Agreement. 
Token Holder, or you means the Person recorded in the Smart Contract ledger as holding Tokens issued under this Agreement, whose Tokens remain outstanding and have not been redeemed or cancelled, and who subsequently have an outstanding Investment in the Recipient.
