Side Letter: Guarantee of Revenue Continuity 
To: Investors of FLP I and FLP II  
c/o: REFI HUB VENTURES S.A. 
Date: March 11, 2026  
Subject: Letter of Undertaking – Insurance Transition and Revenue Guarantee for FLP I & 
FLP II 
Dear Investors, 
This side letter serves as a formal commitment by Pivot Green Asset Management PTE 
LTD regarding the insurance status and revenue continuity of the solar asset listed under 
FLP I and FLP II. 
1. Context of Insurance Renewal 
The Company is currently transitioning our portfolio from individual asset policies to a 
comprehensive Master Operational Insurance Program. This consolidation is a deliberate 
operational upgrade designed to secure superior coverage terms and streamlined claims 
management through Tier-1 global underwriters. 
We are currently in the final stages of evaluating proposals from Chubb, Allianz, and AIG. 
This bulk procurement process ensures that FLP I and FLP II will be backed by industry-
leading capacity, specifically covering specialized risks such as machinery breakdown and 
revenue loss due to downtime. 
2. Asset Management Guarantee 
We recognize the importance of risk mitigation to our investors. Therefore, during this 
interim period (March 2026 through May 2026), the Company provides the following 
unconditional guarantees: 
• Revenue Indemnity: In the event of any unforeseen circumstances resulting in 
technical downtime or the stoppage of recurring revenue at the FLP I or FLP II sites, 
the Company shall be fully responsible for the shortfall. 
• Payment Continuity: The Company guarantees that all scheduled monthly 
distributions to investors will remain uninterrupted and will be paid in full and on 
time, regardless of the operational status of the plants during this transition. 
• Liability Coverage: The Company assumes full financial liability for any damages 
that would typically be covered under the standard operational insurance policy until 
the new policy is active.
