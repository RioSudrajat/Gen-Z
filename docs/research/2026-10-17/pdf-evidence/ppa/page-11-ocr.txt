ENVIRONMENTAL ATTRIBUTES Notwithstanding the Generating Facility's presence as a fixture on the Site, the Developer shall assign or sell in its sole discretion, all rights, title and interest associated with or resulting from the development, installation or operation of the Generating Facility or the production, sale, purchase or use of the Energy Output including without limitation all Environmental Incentives, Environmental Attributes and Reporting Rights associated with the Generating Facility. Environmental Attributes and Reponing Rights associated with the energy output of the Generating Facility are vested in the Developer for 95% and in the Client for 5% of the total energy output of the Generating Facility until the expiry or termination of this Agreement. The Parties agree that the energy output of the Generating Facility shall be allocated according to the above vested percentage on each twelve (12)-month period, throughout this Agreement. The Client reserves the rights to exercise their 5% of Environmental Attributes and Reponing Rights associated with the energy output of the System by providing written request, minimally not less than one (l ) month, from the Client to the Developer.. METERING The Developer shall install and maintain a standard revenue quality meter with electronic data acquisition system ("DAS") capabilities at the Generating Facility. The meter shall measure the alternating current output of the Generating Facility on a continuous basis. In the event ofa failure of the electronic meter reading system and until sueh failure has been corrected, the Developer shall be responsible for conducting monthly on-site readings ofthe standard electricity meter to determine the output of the Generating Facility delivered to the Client. Data retrieved from any such meter shall serve as the basis for invoicing the Client for all delivered energy. The Developer shall verify and adjust all DAS data at least once per calendar year based on readings from the foregoing standard meter. The Developer shall preserve all data compiled hereunder for a period of at least two (2) years following the compilation of such data.
