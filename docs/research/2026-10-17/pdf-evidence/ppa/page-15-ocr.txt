Post-Termination Procedures (a) Termination Payments In the event that this Agreement is tenninated by the Developer due to a Client Event of Default, or is tenninatcd by thc Client for any reason other than (i) a Developer Event of Default or (ii) as a result of the Client exercising its Option to Purchase the Generating Facility provided for in Clause 7, the Client shall pay to the Developer the Fair Value plus all other amounts then owing by the Client to the Developer ("FV Payment"). The amount of Fair Value payable shall be as set forth in ANNEX C of the Terms of Engagement and based on the year during which the termination occurs, and such payment shall be made to the Developer within fourteen (14) days from the date of termination of this Agreement failing which an interest calculated in accordance with Clause 6.2(d) will be imposed on the outstanding amount until full payment has been received by the Developer. (b) Removal of Generating Facility Upon the termination of this Agreement by either Party for any reason whatsoever other than as a result of the Client exercising its Option to Purchase the Generating Facility provided for in Clause 7, and provided the full FV Payment has been made within its due date, the Developer shall remove the Generating Facility from the Premises by a mutually convenienl date but in any event not later than one hundred eighty (180) calendar days after thc date of such termination. Ifthe Parties are unable to reach an agreement as to such mutually convenient date, the Developer shall be entitled to remove the Generating Facility by the 180th calendar day after the date oftermination of this Agreement, or if that day is not a Business Day, the next Business Day. In addition, the Client shall provide the Developer with reasonable access to perform such activities and shall also extend to the Developer all reasonable assistance in connection with such removal of the Generating Facility. In the event that an FV Payment or any part of the FV Payment is overdue, the Developer shall have the right to remove the Generating Facility at any date which is after the due date of the FV Payment. The Developer shall be responsible for bearing all costs associated with the removal of the Generating Facility if this Agreement is terminated by the Client due to a Developer Event of Default, or if the Term expires or this Agreement is not renewed at the end of the Term. The Client shall be responsible for bearing all costs associated with the removal of the Generating Facility if this Agreement is terminated by the Client for any other reason other than a Developer Event of Default or by the Developer due to a Client Event of Default before the end of the Term.
