Force Majeure shall include, without limitation, an annual level of direct beam solar resource availability that is less than or equal to ninety per cent (90%) of historical averages as measured by long-term weather data (minimum of five (5) years) collected at the Site and/or other reliable calibrated and appropriate weather station representative of the Site, or air pollution of Pollutant Standards Index of above 100 as published by the National Environment Agency of Singapore for more than thirty (30) consecutive days, caused by forest fires or any other event or similarly cataclysmic occurrence, failure to achieve Commercial Operation within the Installation Perlod due to reasons related to the Utility or any relevant regulat01Y bodies, damages to the Generating Facility caused by third parties who are not the Developer's Personnel or the Client's Personnel, and the erection of new building structures near to the Site which are beyond the Client's control. For the avoidance of doubt, economic hardship of either Party shall not constitute a Force Majeure under this Agreement. In the event ofeither Pany being unable to perform any one or more of its obligations under this Agreement by reason wholly or partly of Force Majeure, then it shall as soon as reasonably practicable give a notice to the other Party of the relevant details of the Force Majeure and of the obligations which it is unable to perform in consequence thereof. Provided that such notice has been duly give, the lelevant obligations of that Party shall be suspended for the duration of the Force Majeure and that Pany shall be entitled to a reasonable extension of time for performing the suspended obligations after the Force Majeure has ceased. Unless otherwise agteed by both Parties in writing, each Party shall continue performing as much of its obligations as reasonably practical in the circumstances, and undertake reasonable altemative means of performance not prevented by the Force Majeure.
