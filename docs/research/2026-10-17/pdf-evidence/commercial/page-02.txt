PROJECT YIELD ALLOCATION 3.1) Assuming the Maximum Supply is fully subscribed, on each Payment Date, the Recipient shall make a payment of the Aggregate Payment amount to the Smart Contract, which is comprised of both;  a) The “Variable Payment Component”, which is equal to 40% of Gross Revenue generated by the entire Project. b) The “Fixed Payment Component”, which is equal to $925.92 per month, regardless of any actual Gross Revenue realized by the Project. c) The Variable Payment Component and the Fixed Payment Component each constitute separate, independent and enforceable contractual payment obligations of the Recipient. d) The Fixed Payment Component constitutes an absolute and unconditional payment obligation of the Recipient and shall be payable in full on each Payment Date regardless of Gross Revenue, Project performance, or operational status. e) The Fixed Payment Component and the Variable Payment Component are cumulative and shall both be satisfied in accordance with this Schedule. Payment of one component shall not reduce, offset, defer or discharge the obligation to pay the other.  3.2)  The Parties agree that the Project shall generate a minimum Aggregate Payment of $2,040 USD for each Payment Date (the "Target Aggregate Payment"). The Target Aggregate Payment corresponds to the expected Yield profile of the Funding Round, equivalent to an annualized yield of approximately 24.5%, or a monthly Yield of 2.04% based on the Maximum Supply of Tokens. 3.3) If, for any Accounting Period, the Aggregate Payment amount derived from the Variable Payment Component and the Fixed Payment Component is less than the Target Aggregate Payment, the Recipient shall make an additional payment to the Smart Contract equal to the difference (the "Revenue Support Payment"). The Revenue Support Payment shall constitute an absolute and unconditional payment obligation of the Recipient and shall form part of the Aggregate Payment for the relevant Payment Date. 3.4)  The Recipient shall maintain a ledger of all Revenue Support Payments made during the Term (the "Support Balance"). If, for any subsequent Accounting Period, the Aggregate Payment derived from Project Gross Revenue exceeds the Target Aggregate Payment, the excess amount (the "Excess Revenue") may be applied toward reducing the Support Balance.
