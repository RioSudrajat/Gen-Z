This Platform Infrastructure Fee shall be equal to $3000 USDC regardless of final Investment Amount. 4.2) The Platform shall receive a Platform Servicing Fee during each Aggregate Payment, from the First Repayment Date until the End of Term, inclusive of both dates. This Platform Servicing Fee shall be equal to 12.5% of each component of the Aggregate Payment delivered by the Recipient to the Smart Contract during each Payment Date and shall be automatically deducted by the Smart Contract on each Payment Date.  4.3)  Neither of these fees shall constitute custody or discretionary control by the Platform in any way. 5.
